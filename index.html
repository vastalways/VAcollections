Terms & Conditions

1. Payment Terms and Order Processing
  • Deposit Requirement: We require a 50% down payment (DP) to process your order and reserve your items.
  • Same-Day Shipping: If you request shipping within the day, customer must pay the full amount immediately.
  • Future Shipping: For scheduled future delivery, the customer pay the 50% DP upfront.
  • Full Clearance: The customer must settle the remaining 50% balance before the items are shipped.
  • No Pay, No Ship: Vast Always will not release any items for delivery without full payment completion.
2. Cancellation and Holding Period
  • Non-Refundable Deposit: The 50% down payment is strictly non-refundable if you decide to cancel your order after processing.
  • 7-Day Reservation Limit: If you request a later delivery date, we can only hold your items for a maximum of 7 calendar days from the agreed shipping date.
  • Forfeiture: Failure to settle the remaining balance and claim the items within this 7-day period will forfeit your 50% DP, and the items will be returned to our active stock.
3. Shipping Fees and Couriers
  • Customer Responsibility: The customer must shoulder all delivery fees.
  • Same-Day Couriers: Same-day deliveries are booked via standard on-demand apps (e.g., Grab, Lalamove).
  • Standard Couriers: Nationwide shipping is handled via traditional couriers (e.g., J&T Express, LBC).
  • Delivery Failures: If a delivery fails because the customer is unreachable or provides an incorrect address, the customer must shoulder the shipping fee for the second delivery attempt.
  • Courier Liability Disclaimer: Vast Always is not liable for delivery delays, losses, or rider mishandling caused entirely by third-party courier services once the package leaves our premises.
4. No Size Exchanges or Change of Mind
  • Finality of Orders: All sales are final. We do not accept size exchanges or returns due to a change of mind.
  • Customer Responsibility: Customers are fully responsible for checking their orders before checking out. A Size Guide is provided on our website for your reference.
5. Damage, Missing, or Incomplete Items
  • Delivery Confirmation: The customer must confirm receipt on our platform as soon as the courier tags your package as delivered.
  • Strict 24-Hour Window: The customer must report any damage, missing items, or packing errors within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Unboxing Video Requirement: The customer must record a continuous, unedited video of the unboxing process starting from the completely sealed package.
  • Photo Evidence: You must provide clear, close-up photos of any claimed damaged items.
  • Claim Rejection: Vast Always cannot process replacements, store credits, or refunds without a valid unboxing video and clear photos sent within the 24-hour window.
6. Returns and Resolutions
  • Inspection Process: Our team will review your submitted video and photo evidence within 2 to 3 business days.
  • Item Replacement: If the item is proven damaged or incorrect due to our fault, we will replace it for free, subject to stock availability.
  • Store Credit or Refund: If the item is out of stock, the customer will receive store credit or a full refund for that specific item.
  • Shipping Cost for Returns: Vast Always will shoulder the return shipping cost only if the mistake or damage is proven to be from our end.
7. Official Communication Channel
  • Messenger Only: All official customer support, receipt submissions, and order updates happen exclusively through Vast Always Messenger.
  • Order Finalization: Our system will automatically redirect you to Messenger to finish your order.
  • External Channels: We do not honor transactions, receipts, or agreements made outside our official Messenger chat.
